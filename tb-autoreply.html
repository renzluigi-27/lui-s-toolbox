Office hours and leave replies —
set once, forget it.

Auto-replies for Thunderbird with a shared weekly office-hours schedule and a dedicated on-leave mode. One reply per sender a day — no spammed inboxes.

Not yet on Thunderbird Add-ons — currently pending review on ATN. Install manually for now.

What it does

Two modes, one less thing to remember.

🕒

Office Hours mode

One shared weekly schedule — toggle any day off, set start/end times, and it auto-replies outside those hours.

🌴

On Leave mode

Pick one account and a date range. Leave replies override Office Hours automatically while active.

👥

Multi-account

Office Hours applies to every account you check in. Leave mode covers one account at a time.

🔁

No inbox spam

Each sender gets one auto-reply every 24 hours, no matter how many emails they send.

✏️

Rich text messages

Write your Office Hours and Leave messages with a proper editor — separate message per mode.

🔒

100% local

Runs inside Thunderbird only. No external server, no data leaves your machine.

How it works

Simple as 1, 2, 3.

1

Install the .xpi

Early access — download above and load it into Thunderbird manually. Store listing goes live once ATN review is done.

2

Set up Office Hours or Leave

Open the extension's settings, choose accounts, set your schedule or leave dates, and write your message.

3

Save & Activate

Hit Save and Activate — auto-replies start working right away, no restart needed.

🔐

Your inbox stays yours.

TB AutoReply runs entirely inside Thunderbird. It only reads accounts and messages needed to send replies — nothing is uploaded, nothing is sent to any server.
